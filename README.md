# Nikita Spa – Premium Luxury Website

A bespoke, conversion-oriented luxury spa website crafted for **Nikita Spa**, located in **Koramangala, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India**.

Built with modern high-performance technologies: **React 18**, **TypeScript**, **Vite**, **Tailwind CSS**, **React Router v6**, **Lucide React**, and **React Helmet Async**.

## 📱 Features & Highlights

1. **Multi-Page Experience:**
   - **Home (`/`):** Hero with Koramangala location tag, Introduction, 7 Core Treatment Cards, Why Choose Us, Gallery Preview, Editorial Testimonial Placeholders, Location & Google Maps, FAQ Accordion, Final CTA.
   - **About (`/about`):** Nikita Spa’s philosophy of calm, commitment to hygiene, authentic touch, and unhurried care.
   - **Services (`/services`):** Full breakdown of treatments with search, categories, benefits, who it's recommended for, prep instructions, and WhatsApp booking CTAs.
   - **Gallery (`/gallery`):** Filterable gallery with full-screen lightbox modal for treatment suites, reception, and rituals.
   - **Contact (`/contact`):** Interactive booking inquiry form, click-to-call, WhatsApp concierge link, Google Maps embed, and directions.
2. **High-Conversion Mobile System:**
   - Sticky header with clean responsive drawer.
   - Floating WhatsApp button with notification tooltip.
   - Mobile-only sticky bottom booking bar (`Call Now`, `WhatsApp`, `Book Slot`).
   - Interactive appointment booking modal on all pages.
3. **Local SEO & Schema.org:**
   - Pre-configured `DaySpa` / `LocalBusiness` JSON-LD structured data.
   - Semantic HTML5 structure with optimized headings (`H1`, `H2`, `H3`).
   - Open Graph & Twitter meta tags via `React Helmet Async`.
   - `public/robots.txt` and `public/sitemap.xml`.
   - Local keyword targeting for Koramangala and Bengaluru.
